Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the intended handling of information by Eternals, an independent personal visual discovery project.

1. Introduction

Eternals is an independent personal project developed for visual content discovery and organization. This policy explains how information may be processed when interacting with the project and its integrations.

Eternals is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially associated with Pinterest.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features used and the permissions granted, Eternals may process the following information:

  • OAuth authorization codes used during authentication.
  • Access tokens required for authorized API requests.
  • Public Pinterest account information made available by the API.
  • Pin and board information returned by authorized API endpoints.
  • Image URLs and related visual metadata.

Eternals does not request or collect Pinterest account passwords.

3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through authorized integrations is intended to support the project's visual discovery and organization features.

  • Authenticate authorized API requests.
  • Retrieve information permitted by granted scopes.
  • Display available visual resources.
  • Organize image references.

4. Pinterest API

Eternals may integrate with the Pinterest API using OAuth 2.0 authorization.

Access to Pinterest information is subject to the permissions granted by the user, the capabilities of the API and Pinterest's applicable policies.

Eternals does not claim unrestricted access to Pinterest accounts, Pins, boards or private information.

For additional information, please consult Pinterest's Privacy Policy .

5. Data Storage and Security

The public informational website does not provide account registration or a form for submitting personal information.

Authentication credentials and API tokens used by the separate local application must be protected in its execution environment and must not be exposed in public repositories or shared with unauthorized parties.

No specific retention period or permanent storage system is established by this informational website. The handling of application tokens depends on the implementation and configuration of the local tool.

6. Third-Party Services

Eternals may interact with third-party platforms through their official APIs. Those platforms operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Eternals does not control the independent privacy practices of external services.

7. Data Sharing

Eternals is designed as a personal-use project. It is not designed to sell personal information or provide user data to advertising companies.

Information is only intended to be transmitted where necessary to operate authorized integrations and their associated services.

8. User Rights and Authorization

Users can manage or revoke application permissions through the relevant third-party platform settings.

Revoking authorization may prevent Eternals from accessing information through that integration.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in the project's features, integrations or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
